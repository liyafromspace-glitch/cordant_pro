# Cordant demo cheat sheet

## Eight-minute sequence

1. **Frame:** revoked off-chain does not guarantee revoked on-chain.
2. **Lineage:** person → Okta → Fireblocks → key → Solana authority.
3. **Diff:** Ethereum and Arbitrum match; Solana does not.
4. **Replay:** ticket closed while the Solana step remained missing.
5. **AI:** verified, inferred and unknown remain visibly different.
6. **Blast radius:** connect the control failure to $84.2M exposure.
7. **Package:** Cordant compiles a read-only, reviewable response.
8. **Approval:** two people attest to the immutable package hash.
9. **Verification:** Cordant independently observes finality and re-tests policy.
10. **Close:** continuous authority assurance across the portfolio.

## One-sentence thesis

> Cordant can become the evidence layer that connects organizational identity and policy to material operational authority, uses AI to investigate without hiding uncertainty, and closes controls only after independent verification.

## Three-minute compression

- 30 sec: problem and hypothesis.
- 45 sec: lineage, diff and timeline contradiction.
- 35 sec: verified/inferred/unknown AI.
- 35 sec: package and hash-bound approval.
- 25 sec: independent verification.
- 10 sec: portfolio thesis.

## Never overclaim

Say **“product hypothesis”**, **“could”** and **“I would validate.”** Avoid saying that Cordant currently supports blockchain authority reconciliation.
